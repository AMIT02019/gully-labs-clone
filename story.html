Our Story

Hand-lasting, honest materials and small runs — what goes into each pair.

Shaped by hand, not by a mould alone

Every pair in this studio collection starts the same way: leather is cut, stretched and nailed over a wooden last — a foot-shaped form — and left to take its shape before the sole is attached. It is slower than factory moulding, and that is the point. A hand-lasted shoe breaks in around the wearer instead of asking the wearer to break it in.

The lasts, leathers and stitching are worked by small teams of shoemakers. Small runs mean a colourway can sell out and not return, and each pair carries the small variations that come from being made by hand.

Materials that age with you

Full-grain leathers, suedes and heavyweight cotton twills are chosen so they develop character with wear: creases, a softened collar, a deeper tone. The product pages list the exact materials for each item, so you can judge a pair on what it is made of, not just how it photographs.

Getting the size right

Footwear here follows standard UK sizing. If you are between sizes or have a wider foot, sizing up is the safer choice — the full conversion table is on the size guide. Apparel in this catalogue uses S to XL, with trouser waist sizes shown alongside the letter size.

Global shipping

Next-day dispatch

In-stock orders are packed and dispatched the next working day.

30-day returns & exchanges

Unused items can be returned or swapped within 30 days of delivery.

Cash on delivery

Pay at your door on eligible orders — no advance payment needed.
