Shipping

In-stock orders are dispatched the next working day. Delivery is free on orders of ₹5,000 and above; below that, a flat delivery fee applies at checkout. Cash on delivery is available on eligible orders. International delivery times vary by destination and are shown once an order ships.

Returns & exchanges

Unused items in their original condition can be returned or exchanged within 30 days of delivery. To start one, use the contact form below with your order number and the item you want to swap — on this demo site that form only shows a confirmation message and sends nothing anywhere.

Footwear size guide

These sneakers are made true to standard UK shoe size. If you fall between sizes or have a wider foot, we recommend sizing up by half a size.
