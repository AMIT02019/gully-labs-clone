The Indian Shoemaking Renaissance

The Art of Hand-Lasting

Unlike mass-manufactured molded sneakers, each Gully Labs shoe is pulled, tensioned, and hammered over an anatomical wooden last by master Indian artisans. This ancient technique allows the leather to memorize the contours of your feet for lifelong comfort.

Full-Grain Leather

Sourced ethically from historic Indian tanneries, developing a bespoke patina with wear.

Cupsole Construction

Sidewall-stitched durable rubber soles that never delaminate under Indian monsoon conditions.

Anatomical Insole

Custom memory-foam and natural cork composite that cushions every street stride.

Indian Subcultures

Every colorway carries our vernacular DNA — from filter coffee to Himalayan flora.

Footwear Size Guide

Gully Labs sneakers are crafted true to standard UK shoe size. If you fall between sizes or have a wider foot, we recommend sizing up by half a size.

UK / INDIA US MEN EU FOOT LENGTH (CM)
UK 6 US 7 EU 40 25.0 cm
UK 7 US 8 EU 41 25.8 cm
UK 8 US 9 EU 42 26.7 cm
UK 9 US 10 EU 43 27.5 cm
UK 10 US 11 EU 44 28.3 cm
UK 11 US 12 EU 45 29.2 cm
How to measure: Stand on a sheet of paper against a wall. Trace the outline of your heel to your longest toe. Measure the distance in centimeters and compare with the table above.
