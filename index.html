The Art of Hand-Lasting

Unlike mass-manufactured molded sneakers, every pair of Gully Labs shoes is pulled, shaped, and tensioned over bespoke wooden lasts by master artisans in our New Delhi workshop. This ancient lasting process allows the premium leather to mold directly to the contours of your feet for enduring comfort.
